# Student User Guide

## 1. Choose your subject
Enter the subject in the sidebar.

## 2. Choose your level
Select Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

## 3. Add your goal
Example: `Understand Operations Management from basic to advanced with cases.`

## 4. Upload material
Add PDF, DOCX, TXT, or Markdown study material and click Build Knowledge Base.

## 5. Ask naturally
Examples:

- `Explain capacity planning from basic to advanced.`
- `Use my uploaded notes to explain this topic.`
- `Create 10 practice questions.`
- `Evaluate my answer: ...`
- `Give me a case study on this concept.`
- `Teach me Socratically.`
- `Make revision notes.`
- `Create flashcards.`
- `Make a 7-day study plan.`
- `Prepare me for an exam.`

The Manager Agent chooses the workflow.
